# Daily post-close statistics in stock and market wave PNGs

The standalone wave report now calculates descriptive statistics from the currently queried completed daily history. Both entry points and clean/watermarked variants share the same model. The image remains 1284×2778 and uses 「說明」 in place of 「白話」.

The new chapter replaces the existing explanatory chapter when measurements are available. Stock reports compare 20/60-session price changes against the same TAIEX dates; these require 21/61 closes. Three consecutive groups of five completed candles describe the high/low range and mean volume. This is a fixed-window description, not a complete VCP detector. Market reports also calculate the last three sessions' advancing share of the ordinary TWSE stocks covered by the official snapshots, including flat stocks in the denominator. TPEx is not mixed into TAIEX breadth.

Report export loads `data/index/TAIEX.json` and the last three `data/market/archive/YYYY-MM-DD.json` files using the verified calendar and queried date. These are already produced by the existing scheduled market/index publication workflows. No historical example, stock name, expected percentage or specific date is embedded in the production calculator. Completed evidence is reused for up to five minutes and across clean/watermarked exports; incomplete fetches are retried on the next export. Each request has an eight-second abort timeout. The browser retains at most two dates of supplementary evidence.

Wrong-date/missing benchmarks, missing market snapshots, calendar gaps, unresolved corporate actions and volume conflicts suppress only the affected measurements. Omitted measurements do not get placeholder rows. If no new statistic is usable, the new chapter is not created and the original explanatory view remains. Existing data warnings and quote guards remain authoritative. New observations do not change eligibility, ranking, scores, original price levels, personal stops or wave counts.

Price comparisons use the source's unadjusted prices and are labelled as observations rather than total returns or buy signals. Three-day market percentages use each day's reported sample size; the full counts and source dates remain in the canvas audit. A stock that falls less than the index is described as 「少跌」, never automatically as bullish. A pattern that does not contract returns its actual values and an explicit negative finding.

Validation: 13 new unit cases cover actual archived 4743/TAIEX arithmetic, 60-bar partial availability, gaps, duplicate dates, wrong-date benchmarks, zero/conflicting volume, unresolved adjustments, true zero values, breadth coverage and negative-cache recovery. The browser test replays actual daily data through production canvases for four clean/watermarked outputs, the legacy market adapter and the independent market entry. It checks dimensions, clipping, card bounds, 「說明」 wording, exact sample counts and no uncaught errors. Existing unit, contract, sharing, download and image-mode regression suites continue to run. The deployment workflow also runs the new browser replay before deployment and after publication.
